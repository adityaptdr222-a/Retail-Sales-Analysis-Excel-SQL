# Retail Sales Analysis (Excel + SQL)

## Objective
Analyze retail sales data to understand product-wise and region-wise performance and identify improvement opportunities using Excel and SQL.

## Dataset
- Retail sales transactional data
- Contains product, region, revenue, cost, and profit information

## Tools Used
- Excel (Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Charts)
- SQL (SELECT, GROUP BY, SUM, profit analysis)
- Business analysis techniques

## Analysis Performed
- Product-wise revenue and profit analysis
- Region-wise performance comparison
- Identification of top-performing and weak products
- Identification of high-performing and weak regions

## Key Insights
- iPhone is the highest revenue-generating product
- North and South regions contribute the most to total revenue
- Headphones is the weakest performing product
- West is the weakest performing region

## Business Recommendations
- Increase focus and inventory on high-performing products in top regions
- Improve marketing and pricing strategy for weak products and regions

-This project includes Excel and SQL analysis to identify
top and weak products and regions based on revenue and profit.
